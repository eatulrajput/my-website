Setting priorities is essential. Why? Because when you’re young, you often think you have plenty of time. You believe you can do everything you see and explore every new trend. Using the internet isn’t inherently bad — in fact, it can be a powerful tool — but the most important thing is your work. The completion of that work must be the top priority because it puts food on your table and sustains you.

You can’t do everything at once. Maybe you see a new buzzword in tech, get excited, and dive into it. But meanwhile, your actual work — what should truly be your priority — is being neglected. This is how distraction takes over. You begin one task, get enticed by something else, and shift your focus. You start another course, abandon the previous one, and lose sight of what really matters. You end up with many unfinished projects and no clear progress.

So, what’s the solution to this rabbit hole?

Before starting anything new, ask yourself:

- Why do I want to do this?
- What will I gain from it?
- How much time do I need to dedicate to reach a certain level?
- What will I lose if I quit halfway?

Only when you can clearly answer these questions can you confidently invest your time. Remember, time is your most valuable resource. The goal is not just to learn, but to learn deeply enough to apply that knowledge — especially in interviews or real-world situations.

That’s how you break the loop: by setting clear priorities and holding yourself accountable. That’s the real solution.
