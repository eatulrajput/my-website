> Many came, many went like air,
> just gone, as they came.
> You will be gone soon as others,
> doesn’t matter anymore —
> life goes as it is.
>
> What matters is that I’m on the way
> to find the self within,
> where I live with reality.
>
> Thanks to all who came.
> Learned a lot,
> if tried to understand
> with the objective lens of people.
> Thanks to you.
