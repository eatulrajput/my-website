> Fallen asleep since birth,
> Cradled by a slumbering world.
> When you wake,
> it may not be the best time,
> May not be the peaceful night,
> May not be what you wanted-
> May not be the world you hoped for.
>
> But when you are awake,
> You hold the truth-
> Eternal, peripheral,
> A light beneath the surface.
> Beneficial, not just to you,
> But to the world itself.
