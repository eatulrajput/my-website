Instagram Is Not Needed And the Most Powerful People in Tech Prove It
</BlogTitle>


<CoverImage src="/images/blog/instagram/instagram.webp" />

<BlogContent>
There’s a lie floating around modern culture that refuses to die:

<Quote>
“If you’re not on Instagram (or social media), you’re missing out.”
</Quote>

That lie is repeated mostly by people who need attention to survive, not by people who build things that matter. Let’s get something straight. The internet was not built by influencers. It was built by people you’ve never seen on your feed.

## The People Who Actually Run Tech Are Invisible
The creators of Linux, Python, C, UNIX, Git, core cryptography systems, networking protocols, compilers, databases; they didn’t grow audiences. They grew infrastructure.

Linus Torvalds doesn’t post stories. Guido van Rossum doesn’t chase engagement. Dennis Ritchie never “built a personal brand.” Yet the world runs on their work. That alone should end the conversation.

## Instagram Rewards the Opposite of What Tech Demands
Real tech excellence requires:
- Deep focus
- Long attention spans
- Boredom tolerance
- Intellectual humility
- Years of invisible effort

Instagram trains:
- Dopamine addiction
- Shallow thinking
- Constant comparison
- Performative productivity
- Fake urgency

These two mindsets are fundamentally incompatible. You cannot debug complex systems while your brain is trained to crave novelty every 8 seconds.

## “But Networking!”
This is where the cope gets loud. High-level tech networking happens through: Code contributions, Research, Shipped products, Reputation, Referrals, Conferences, One-on-one respect, Not reels. Not likes. Not aesthetics.

If your career depends on strangers tapping a heart icon, you’re not in tech, you’re in entertainment.

## Social Media Confuses Visibility With Value
Instagram is a visibility machine, not a value machine.

It makes people: Look busy without being effective, Feel productive without producing, Appear successful without competence,

In tech, visibility without substance is worse than useless, it’s a red flag. Quiet competence beats loud mediocrity every time.

## The Harsh Truth No One Wants to Say
Instagram is not neutral. It actively:

Steals attention
Fragments thinking
Encourages ego over mastery
Turns life into a performance
And worst of all — it convinces people they’re “falling behind” while they’re being held back.

Most people don’t fear missing opportunities. They fear sitting alone with their own potential.

Who Actually Needs Instagram? Let’s be honest. Instagram is useful if:
- You sell attention
- You sell lifestyle
- You monetize perception
- You depend on validation

It is not required to:
- Build software
- Do research
- Become elite at anything difficult
- Earn respect in serious technical circles
- The higher you go in real tech, the quieter it gets.

## Final Verdict
Instagram is optional for life. Irrelevant for tech. And often a liability for serious thinkers.

If someone tells you “you need Instagram to succeed,” ask one question:

<Quote>
Succeed at what — being seen, or being valuable?
</Quote>

Because the people who actually move the world don’t post about it. They’re too busy building it.
