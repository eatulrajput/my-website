Aaj 18th Apr, 2026 hai, aur aaj college premises me mera aakhri din hai. Kucch krte hai, kucch likhte hai aaj…

Aaya to tha kucch aur viccharo ko lekar ish college mein, kucch sapne thae mere, kucch umeede thi meri baaki logo ki tarah.

To fir chaliye, suru krte hai… Technical Guruji yaad aaye kya?

Mai ek school se tha, ek society se tha, jaha competition, comparison, ek dusre se har waqt aage niklne ki race lag rehti hai. Jish wazah se mera scepticism peak point pe rehta hai. Mai in sab chizo se tang ho chuka tha, dukhi rehta tha.

Yes, death is absolute truth. Aur wo dikha mujhe, suspasth tha, mere saamne tha. Wo din aur raat, kaafi… pira dene waali thi. Kucch samajh nhi aa rha tha, ek baat spasth to ho hi gaya tha ki ek baar jab Death se dosti hogayi to fir Life se dosti tut jayega.

Jab Death ne gate knock kiya tha, koshish ki thi usne, mere parents se dosti krne ki, lekin fir uska man badal gaya sayad.

Tab ek lesson to jarur sikha tha, ant hi antim satya hai, wo satya jo sabse karvi hai, itni karvi hai ki log ushe bhulaa dete hai.

But, society aur sonch badlna aasaan to bilkul nhi hai, koi kishi ko badal nhi sakta, koi kishi ko kucch sikha bhi nhi sakta, aur ye tab tak hoga jab tak, sikhne wala ye maan na le ki wo sikhna, samjhna, jan ne ki abhilasha aur jigyasa rakhta ho, aur sath hi sath ye bhi accept krle ki ushe kucch bhi pata nhi hai. Ha tabhi to wo question kr sakega, tabhi to wo ek nyi sonch ko samajhne ki koshish krega, naa ki comparison mein lage rhe apni pre-dominating nature ko leke ladai krte rhe, ishe backfire effect kehte hai.

To fir covid mere ghar se ravana hua, maine JEE ke failures ka haste, rote, daat sunte saamna kiya.

Fir drop year hua, fir ek aur lesson sikha, jo ham sonchte hai, jaruri nhi hai ki plan ke hisaab se wo sab kucch ho. Fir kya, maine jaise planning kri thi waisa kucch to hua nhi, mai koi naadaan baalak to tha nhi, bas thori samajh ki kami thi, right guidance ki kami wale excuse ke layer mein wrap krke apne aap ko bacchane ki koshish kyu kru mai…

Internet to tha hi mere paas, google search pe to saare answers mil bhi jaate agar sahi sawaal khoje jate. Lekin ush vyakti ko kya pata tha ki wo jo question kr rha hai, wo sawaal hi galat hai, wo sawaal uske andar se aaya bhi nhi hai, wo to ush society mein rehne ke wajah usme man mein wo sawal aaya hai. - How to think krte kaise hai…
- Question krna koi paap nhi hai…

- Aur galatiyo se sikh leke aage badh jana, naaki regret mein dube rehna…

- Paise kamao, khub paise kaamao…

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry padho, khub padho, ishliye padho kyuki tum unhe khudse samajhna chahte ho, taki tum un subjects ko enjoy krte ho padhte time, unke logics tumhe lubhate hai, unke molecules questions tumhe sapne mein dikhte hai.

Ishliye mat padho ki tumhe exam pass krna hai, ya exam mein acche ank laane hai. Acche se samajhna hi padhai krna hota hai, acche number laane ka matlab ye hota hai ki concepts aapko 100% clear hai, naki rote learning krke number le aao.

Some argue that Biology is a rote learning subject. But I disagree with you here, it is an understandable concept based subject, theory to practice based. The more you try to better understand it, you will understand that more easily. May be you did not get the best teacher who knows how to teach. Memorization is different from rote learning. First understand the concept, raise doubts until understand, then find answers from wherever possible. Then you will understand.

Kishine bataya hi nhi ki life goals kyu hone chahiye.. Un goals ke purposes kya hai, kya mil jayega achieve krne se, kishi ne life ko samajhne hi nhi diya, life hoti kya, ishe jeene ki sahi saleeka kya hai..
